Questions?

How long does delivery take? +

We ship within 2 business days. Standard shipping takes 3-5 days within the continental US. International orders ship within 5-7 days with tracking.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes! Cancel your subscription anytime with no penalties. Simply manage it in your account or contact our support team for assistance.

Are coffees ethically sourced? +

100% ethically sourced and fairly traded. We work directly with farmer cooperatives ensuring fair compensation and sustainable practices throughout.

What's the shelf life? +

Best enjoyed within 3-4 weeks of roasting. All packages show the roast date. Store in an airtight container away from light and heat for optimal freshness.

Gift subscriptions available? +

Yes! Perfect for coffee lovers. Choose any plan and we'll send a beautiful gift card with brewing instructions and tasting notes for each shipment.
